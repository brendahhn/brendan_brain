# Decision: personal-care setup is locked (Brendan-confirmed 2026-09-07)

Brendan confirmed this setup verbatim ("Valid? Yep") and read back the full list. It is
authoritative until he supersedes it. Two standing principles run through it:

- **Fragrance-free by default** on anything that touches skin or scalp.
- **Finish before replacing.** A named successor is not a shopping-list item until the current
  product is nearly empty. No backups "just in case", no matching-brand add-ons.

## Locked core (in use / buy now)

| Slot | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shampoo | Odele Ultra Sensitive Shampoo (beige bottle, fragrance-free) | Scalp-first, runoff cleans the lengths. Weekly-ish, or when scalp is oily/dirty/product-heavy. Less often if hair goes too straight. |

| Styling | Existing product | Unchanged. |

## Sunscreen

- **Everyday face: EltaMD UV AOX Elements Tinted SPF 50.** Target probably won't stock it —
  **order the exact product online; do not substitute a random tinted SPF to finish a trip.**
- **Secondary / test: Australian Gold Botanical Tinted Face Mineral SPF 50** — face, ears, neck,
  backs of hands, and any skin outside the wetsuit.
- **Lips: Supergoop PLAY Lip Shield SPF 30, Strawberry.** Buy one and test it first. If Target
  doesn't carry that exact one, any reputable fragrance-tolerable SPF 30+ balm is fine for the
  work pouch.
- **Sun Bum coconut lip balm is removed from the plan** (explicit, 2026-09-07).
- Keep sunscreen in its original packaging where practical.

## Do NOT buy (standing, until Brendan says otherwise)

New vitamin C serum (finishing the current one, then several weeks off to test whether it matters);
new hyaluronic-acid serum (already has one, and Vanicream contains HA); retinol (finish current);
Paula's Choice 2% BHA (wait until actually ready to introduce it); azelaic acid (wait for 6–8 weeks
of a stable, comfortable routine); Differin (not in the plan unless significant acne becomes the
main problem); face scrub; toner; eye cream; body lotion unless currently dry; conditioner bought
to match the shampoo; scented "sensitive" body wash; another deodorant.

## Routine

**Morning** — 1. rinse, or COSRX cleanser only if needed → 2. Vanicream → 3. EltaMD tinted SPF 50
→ 4. lip SPF.

**Night** — 1. oil cleanser only when needed for stubborn sunscreen/grime → 2. CeraVe now, COSRX
after it empties → 3. treatment per schedule → 4. Vanicream.

**Treatment schedule** — Mon retinol · Tue recovery · Wed shave · Thu BHA · Fri recovery ·
Sat retinol · Sun recovery. **No BHA on retinol nights. No retinol or BHA on shaving nights.**

**Hair/body** — fresh-water rinse after surfing; Dove Sensitive bar for body; Odele as needed;
existing styling product; Native deodorant.

## Work/travel duplicates (kit build)

Two travel fluoride toothpastes · lip SPF 30+ · small hand sanitizer · pocket tissues · 3M Aura N95

packets · tiny travel jars if the existing tubes don't suit hair product. Existing travel tubes
