Generic evidence layer for the "can cold sores and allergies be reversed?" question
(`task-20260723-reverse-cold-sores-dog-cat-food`, health-robot Run 55). Confidence medium —
sourced from meta-analyses and controlled studies, but the personalized allergy answer is still
blocked on Brendan's allergy history (see `q-20260815-allergy-history-for-desensitization`).

**Cold sores (HSV): not curable — the goal is fewer/milder, not gone.** The virus lives dormant
in a nerve for life; nothing eradicates it. Ranked realistic levers:
- **Stress down-regulation** is the strongest non-drug lever. A meta-analysis of prospective
  studies found psychological stress predicts herpes recurrences, and the link is *stronger for
  oral (cold-sore) herpes than genital* — so the breathing / sleep-regularity / gut-directed
  hypnotherapy stack already recommended for stress+gut doubles as a cold-sore lever.
- **Lip SPF before sun** is a cheap, no-downside add: in a controlled UV-challenge study, lip
  sunscreen dropped sun-triggered cold sores from 71% of people to zero. (Experimental sun-exposure
  model → real-world benefit is a reasonable extrapolation, not a hard-outcome trial.)
- **Daily suppressive vs. episodic antiviral** is a prescriber conversation (highest-certainty
  medical lever, but their call).
- **De-hype:** L-lysine has weak, mixed evidence and anything under ~1 g/day is ineffective — not
  a reliable lever.

**Allergy "reversal" = desensitization (immunotherapy), which is slow, partial, and specialist-run
— not a cure.**
- Pet dander (cat/dog): high-dose allergy shots (SCIT) have real but inconsistent benefit.
- **Food — the important correction:** oral immunotherapy (OIT) reliably raises the reaction
  *threshold* but **increases the rate of anaphylaxis vs. simple avoidance (high-certainty meta,
  ~3× the risk)** and rarely produces lasting tolerance once you stop. It's a lifelong-maintenance
  trade, not a cure. True food allergy vs. oral-allergy-syndrome (itchy mouth to raw fruit/nuts,
  fine cooked) must be distinguished first — it decides whether desensitization is even worth
  discussing.

**Immune-drug corollary (for anyone on an S1P-modulator, a class used for some autoimmune
conditions):** these carry a label warning for herpes reactivation, so the cold-sore levers matter
a bit more. Unlike live vaccines (which that class blocks), the shingles vaccine **Shingrix is
non-live and is NOT ruled out** — a "raise it with the prescriber" item. Allergy immunotherapy on
an immune-modulating drug isn't contraindicated but is an individualized specialist call.

Not medical advice; medication and immunotherapy decisions are a prescriber's.
