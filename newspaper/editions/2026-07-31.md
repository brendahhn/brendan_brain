### [CONC] The cheapest stress tool is your own breath — and the mechanism is specific
<sub>source: [`from-health-robot.md`](queue/inbox/from-health-robot.md)</sub>

Run 39 (the first "organism mode" run now that all 36 chapters are done) pulled a quick, well-evidenced item: how to actually do **cyclic sighing**.

- **[A]** The single best-evidenced quick practice for lowering stress is **cyclic sighing** — a **double inhale** (normal nose breath, then a short sip on top) followed by a **long, slow exhale through the mouth**. A 2023 Stanford RCT found 5 min/day for a month beat box breathing, "power/Wim-Hof" hyperventilation, *and* mindfulness on mood and breathing rate. Effect sizes modest, volunteers healthy — but it's free, five minutes, no equipment.
- **[A]** **The exhale is the active ingredient.** An exhale longer than the inhale (~1:2) is what shifts you toward calm. A **single physiological sigh** (1–3 breaths) is the fastest real-time reset for an acute stress or anger spike — keep it in your back pocket before any high-pressure moment.
- **[B]** **Direction matters, and it's an easy mistake:** long-exhale breathing calms you *down*; inhale-emphasized rapid "power breathing" revs you *up*. For stress, the long-exhale version is the tool.
- **[B]** **Common errors:** making the second inhale a whole extra breath (it's a short top-off), rushing the exhale, wrong openings (nose in, mouth out), or forcing it to lightheadedness. Consistency beats intensity.
