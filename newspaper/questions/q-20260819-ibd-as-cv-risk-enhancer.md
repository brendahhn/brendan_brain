One low-stakes question to keep in your back pocket for a future visit — **not urgent, no action now.**

At your next cholesterol / heart-risk check, it's reasonable to have the doctor treat inflammatory bowel
disease as a **"risk-enhancer"** when interpreting the numbers (the way rheumatoid arthritis is treated),
since standard young-adult risk calculators can under-score people with an inflammatory disease. See
`knowledge-20260819-ibd-cardiovascular-risk-activity-dependent` for the evidence base. Optional; surface
once and let it rest.
